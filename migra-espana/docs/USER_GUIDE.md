# User guide
Pick a language (top bar). Take the **eligibility check** (skip what you don't know — unanswered is not "no"). Read each pathway in the **law centre**
(note status, legal reference, last-verified date). Follow the **14-step guide**. Upload to the **vault** (only genuine documents; share with a verified lawyer only with your consent, revoke any time).
Update your **case** status manually or via your lawyer. The AI assistant gives general information with sources — not legal advice. Government fees are paid to the administration, not to us.
